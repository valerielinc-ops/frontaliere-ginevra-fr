Founded in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the world’s oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, faithfully perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timepieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

Internship, ASAP for 6 months

Plan-les-Ouates

FOR THOSE WHO SEEK EXCELLENCE

Join one of the most respected Maisons in fine watchmaking where excellence is both a given and a never-ending quest. Play your part in driving our collective success.

YOUR ROLE

We are seeking an enthusiastic and creative e-Learning Intern to join our International Training team. Under the guidance of the International Training Manager, the intern will support the day-to-day operational execution of our digital learning initiatives for retail and wholesale teams globally.

The intern’s main focus will be on creating engaging, interactive, and gamified content on our mobile learning app (My1755), actively animating the learner community, and ensuring a seamless, fun learning experience across all markets.

PLAYING YOUR PART

CONTENT CREATION & GAMIFICATION

  • You will support the transformation of existing training materials into engaging digital formats
  • You will bring fresh, creative ideas to make learning playful, bite-sized, and memorable
  • You will assist in reviewing, testing, and proofreading content prior to publication to guarantee high quality and consistency with brand tone

COMMUNITY ANIMATION & ENGAGEMENT

  • You will animate the learner community on the My1755 app through regular posts, challenges, news updates, and push notifications
  • You will foster interaction, encourage peer-to-peer sharing, and celebrate learner milestones and achievements
  • You will act as a first-line relay to collect user feedback, learner questions, and relay best practices from the field

OPERATIONAL SUPPORT

  • You will assist with the daily administration of the app, test user journeys and report any technical glitches
  • You will help monitor community activity and extract monthly engagement/completion metrics to feed dashboard reports
  • You will contribute to broader International Training projects and provide hands-on, ad-hoc support to the team whenever operational priorities arise

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU SUCCESSFUL?

Success in this role will require

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree
  • A first internship, ideally in the luxury environment, would be a plus
  • Solid communication, analytical and organizational skills
  • PowerPoint, Excel and Canva proficiency
  • Rigor, curiosity, proactive attitude and teamwork
  • Fluency in English and French

Vacheron Constantin celebrates more than 270 years as the world’s oldest watchmaker in continuous production since 1755. Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, we operate a global retail network of nearly 200 points of sale across 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional global community of watchmakers, artisans and dedicated professionals share a common devotion to our exceptional heritage and a passion to trailblaze an even brighter future.

If this fires your imagination, we welcome your application.
