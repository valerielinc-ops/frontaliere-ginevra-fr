Description complète

Reference code: JR136395

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

CONTEXT

The Europe FP&A controller is responsible for financial planning and analysis of the wholesale business in Europe for a defined scope of Maisons.

This role involves being a key financial partner for Europe Maison Management and Finance teams to support business operations and decision making.

Reporting to Europe FP&A Manager, the position is based in Meyrin, Geneva, within our ‘Campus Genevois de Haute Horlogerie’.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

Europe Wholesale Financial Planning

  • Coordinate the consolidation of the full P&L and Cash Flow items for a defined scope of Maisons
  • Adjust budget figures based on quarterly reviews with the appropriate stakeholders
  • Communicate forecasts and gap analysis to the Maisons HQ

Europe Wholesale Monthly Closing & Reporting Activities

  • Perform analytical reviews on the P&L and Cash Flow items for a defined scope of Maisons and report results with comments to Maisons’ HQ
  • Develop and maintain financial reporting to proactively support Maisons’ decision-making processes
  • Support the Management Reporting process (upload and reconcile actual monthly results into the system for consolidation)
  • Run various checks and corrections to ensure accurate closing in line with IFRS

Operations

  • Act as a true business partner and strengthen the relationship with Maisons and Markets.
  • Ensure continuous improvement of business processes to gain efficiency in the organization
  • Support Richemont Europe Management in various ad-hoc requests
  • Participate actively in controlling ad-hoc projects (e.g. system automation, community management, closing process improvements, digitalization, cash-flow guidance, etc.)

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

You hold a Master’s degree in Finance, Business Administration, or equivalent, with at least 3 years of relevant experience in Finance, ideally in FP&A, controlling or audit. Experience in the luxury industry would be a plus.

You thrive in a complex and fast-paced environment, managing multiple priorities and tight deadlines while consistently delivering thoughtful and high-quality work.

You are results-oriented, with a strong appetite for improving efficiency through automation, digital tools and reporting optimisation.

You have a strong analytical and critical mindset, combined with the ability to quickly understand complex topics and translate data into meaningful insights.

You are a strong team player and communicator, comfortable building relationships with a wide range of internal stakeholders, including Regional Executive Committees.

You enjoy collaborative problem-solving, bring a proactive mindset and are able to develop creative and pragmatic solutions.

You are self-motivated and autonomous, while enjoying working collaboratively within an international and diverse environment.

You have advanced Excel skills and are comfortable working with financial and analytical tools such as SAP, Looker and Copilot. Automation and digital skills are a strong plus.

You are fluent in English; any additional language is an asset.

You are motivated by an international environment with strong exposure to Top Management and eager to further develop your Finance expertise.

Then, you might be the right talent to join our regional team as Junior Europe FP&A Controller.
