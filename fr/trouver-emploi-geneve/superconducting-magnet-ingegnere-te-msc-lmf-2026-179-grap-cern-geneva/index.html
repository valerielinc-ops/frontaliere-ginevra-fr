Description complète

As a superconducting magnet engineer in the Large Magnet Facility (LMF), you will contribute to the development of next-generation high-field accelerator magnets. Working in close collaboration with the Superconducting Magnet Technology section and international partners, you will combine numerical modelling, experimental analysis and hands-on engineering to support the design, fabrication and qualification of Nb3Sn magnet prototypes for future accelerators.

Your responsibilities

  • Contribute to the design and development of high-field superconducting accelerator magnets, with a particular focus on mid-length Nb3Sn prototypes.
  • Perform electromagnetic and mechanical analyses using numerical modelling tools to support magnet design, optimisation and performance assessment.
  • Build on experience and know-how from previous Nb3Sn magnet production and apply it to future magnet technologies.
  • Follow the fabrication, assembly, instrumentation and testing of magnet prototypes, and contribute to the interpretation of experimental results.
  • Participate in R&D activities aimed at improving superconducting magnet technologies through mock-ups, demonstrators and dedicated experimental campaigns.

Your profile

  • Finite-element analysis and numerical modelling of complex engineering systems.
  • Electromagnetic and/or mechanical design of superconducting magnets or comparable high-performance devices.
  • Analysis and interpretation of experimental or instrumentation data.
  • Engineering documentation and technical reporting.
  • Knowledge of superconductivity and superconducting magnet technologies would be an asset.

Skills

  • Knowledge of finite-element and multiphysics simulation tools (e.g. ANSYS, OPERA, ROXIE, COMSOL).
  • Knowledge of electromagnetic and mechanical design principles.
  • Ability to analyse test data and validate numerical models.
  • Scientific communication skills, including technical reporting and presentations.
  • Ability to work effectively in multidisciplinary and international teams.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • You have a professional background in Mechanical Engineering (or a related field) and have either:
  • a Master's degree with 2 to 6 years of post-graduation professional experience;
  • or a PhD with no more than 3 years of post-graduation professional experience.
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
