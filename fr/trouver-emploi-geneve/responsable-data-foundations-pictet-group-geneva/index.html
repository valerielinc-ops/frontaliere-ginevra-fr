Description complète

Your team

Pictet Tech designs, builds, and operates the technology that underpins the Group’s core activities, from portfolio management and trading to banking services. Within Data Platforms , our ambition is to build a modern, resilient, and scalable data foundation across the Group. We are transforming our technology landscape by combining cloud and on-premise strengths, modernizing how data is stored, processed, integrated, and distributed, and raising engineering standards across all domains. As a member of the management team, you will lead around 15 engineers operating and evolving our database, messaging, event streaming and data integration platforms, helping turn complex challenges into secure, reliable, and high-performing capabilities that enable teams to deliver faster and with greater confidence.

Your role

  • Lead a team of around 15 engineers: performance evaluations, continuous feedback, professional growth and career progression.
  • Ensure the availability, resilience and security of critical data and integration platforms, balancing run and change.
  • Drive the team’s transformation towards the cloud, replacing manual operations with automation and self-service.
  • Define the platform roadmap with product teams and architecture, building a culture of customer feedback.
  • Mentor engineers towards modern platform engineering and SRE practices.
  • Contribute to recruitment and foster a positive, collaborative environment that supports engineers through change.

Your profile

  • At least 4 years of IT engineering experience and first experience in a leadership role.
  • Solid background in data platforms or application integration (databases, messaging and event streaming, data integration), ideally in critical, regulated environments.
  • Experience in operational excellence (incident management, resilience) and in leading a team through a transformation such as a cloud migration.
  • Demonstrated expertise in platform engineering best practices: end-to-end responsibility, customer-centricity, automation.
  • Ability to facilitate technical and architectural discussions on complex systems and data flows.
  • Strong understanding of cloud technologies and the software development lifecycle.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Fluent in French and English.
  • Swiss resident or willingness to relocate.
