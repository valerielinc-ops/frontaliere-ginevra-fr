Description complète

Within the HSE Unit, we have the QARE Service. Quality and Aging Research for Cable (QARE) service is a quality control service for cables and a research program on cable ageing assessment at CERN accelerators. The activity involves the development of the most appropriate cable testing techniques to perform quality control on cables procured for CERN, and establish aging requirements on cables operating in the CERN accelerators environment. QARE service ensures quality control and conducts Site Acceptance Tests. In addition, the QARE team develops and performs mechanical and chemical tests for the ageing and radiation-hardness qualification of cables. You will carry out mechanical and chemical testing, manage cable samples throughout irradiation and laboratory testing campaigns, and prepare reports from the obtained results.

Your responsibilities

  • Develop mechanical and chemical test procedures.
  • Test and qualify cables against CERN requirements.
  • Analyse mechanical and chemical results.
  • Define representative samples and exposure locations.
  • Organise and monitor irradiation campaigns.
  • Follow-up measurement accuracy.
  • Install, track, retrieve and manage cable samples.
  • Document results and support quality-control activities.

Your profile

  • Experience in polymer or cable-material characterisation.
  • Knowledge of mechanical, chemical and thermal testing.
  • Understanding of material ageing and radiation effects.
  • Experience with laboratory testing and data analysis.
  • Familiarity with calibration, traceability and quality control.
  • Ability to write test procedures and technical reports.
  • Irradiation testing or dosimetry experience is an advantage.

Skills

  • Excellent knowledge of materials characterisation.
  • Good knowledge of polymer and cable material characterisation.
  • Tensile, elongation, and hardness testing.
  • FTIR spectroscopy, DSC, and TGA.
  • Material ageing and radiation degradation.
  • Quality control and qualification process.
  • Writing procedures and test reports.
  • Python and Microsoft Excel for data analysis.
  • Laboratory data-acquisition software.
  • Some experience in chemical or mechanical laboratories.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • By the application deadline, you have a maximum of two years of professional experience since graduation in Materials Engineering (or a related field) and your highest educational qualification is either a Bachelor's or Master's degree .
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.
  • Applicants without University degree are not eligible.
  • Applicants with a PhD are not eligible.
