Description complète

Your team

Pictet Trading & Sales (PTS) provides direct access to global markets, offering bespoke trading solutions across a wide range of asset classes. Our sales traders, IT specialists, strategists and quant analysts are assisted by a full suite of risk and control functions as well as the latest technology.

PTS is seeking a talented Data Engineer (AI Projects) to join our dynamic Data Office team. In this role, you'll transform complex data challenges into innovative technology solutions that propel our business forward, building both the data products and the agentic AI capabilities of PTS. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and implement impactful solutions, enhance our data infrastructure and help deploy AI agents across the business in a safe and controlled way. Located on the trading floor in Geneva, our office fosters a collaborative environment where innovation thrives. You'll also work with teams in Luxembourg, Montreal, and Singapore. If you're passionate about data and technology, join us to shape the future of PTS.

Your role

  • Participate in and manage projects within the PTS Data Office.
  • Design and implement data products and technology solutions using DevOps/DataOps best practices.
  • Contribute to the deployment of agentic AI at PTS: identify use cases with business teams, then design, build and operate AI agents that augment trading, sales and operational workflows.
  • Build the data foundations for AI agents: governed data access, retrieval pipelines (RAG) and tool/API integrations (e.g. MCP).
  • Implement evaluation, monitoring and guardrails for AI agents in line with the bank’s risk and compliance requirements.
  • Enhance the team’s infrastructure with new tools to effectively track and monitor data architecture, workflows and analytics processes.
  • Promote AI adoption across PTS by sharing best practices and supporting colleagues in using AI and data tools effectively.

Your profile

  • Master’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Physics, or deemed equivalent (e.g. EPFL/ETHZ).
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in SQL-coding (SQL Server), and familiarity with modern data management tools (ETL, Object storage, Git, Liquibase, DevOps/DataOps).
  • Proficiency in full-stack development of web applications using React and adherence to software development best practices.
  • Strong knowledge in Python or similar programming language and BI tools (e.g. Tableau).
  • Hands-on experience building LLM-based applications and AI agents (e.g. LangGraph, Claude Agent SDK, OpenAI Agents SDK), including RAG, tool calling and evaluation.
  • Understanding of AI risks and governance in a regulated environment (data privacy, model risk, human oversight).
  • Familiarity with FIX would be a definite advantage.
  • Keen interest in the trading and financial markets.
  • Excellent command of French and English, both written and spoken.
  • The successful candidate will be required to reside in Switzerland or be willing to relocate.

As the chosen applicant, you will be an excellent communicator and listener as well as adaptable. Moreover, you will have strong analytical skills, be client- and solutions-oriented, and have a genuine curiosity for emerging AI technologies. You will also be keen to join a dynamic team where you will be able to put your skills as a team player to excellent use. Your pragmatism and ability to work under stress will likewise be valuable attributes that will enable you to accomplish the various tasks assigned to you.
