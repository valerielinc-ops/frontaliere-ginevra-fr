Job Title

ERP Project Intern - Finance

Department

Finance

Location

Geneva

Job Description

The ERP Project Finance intern will support the Finance department in a strategic project to select and implement a new ERP system for the bank. Working closely with Finance, IT and Project Management, the intern will help gather and structure requirements, analyse ERP solutions, support vendor evaluations, contribute to project documentation and coordination, and, after selection, participate in the preparation and early stages of the ERP implementation.

Top of Form

Main Responsibilities

  • Project & Coordination Support
  • Assist the project manager and Finance lead in planning and tracking project activities, milestones and deliverables.
  • Prepare and maintain project documentation (meeting minutes, action logs, status reports, presentations).
  • Finance Requirements & Process Analysis
  • Support the collection and documentation of Finance requirements (e.g. general ledger, AP/AR, fixed assets, consolidation, reporting).
  • Help map and document current Finance processes and contribute to defining target (“to be”) processes.
  • Assist in structuring and prioritising requirements into clear lists or matrices.
  • ERP & Vendor Evaluation Support
  • Contribute to the preparation of RFI/RFP materials (questionnaires, requirement catalogues, evaluation criteria).
  • Help collect and compare information on ERP vendors and solutions from a Finance perspective (functional coverage, reporting, controls, integration with banking systems).
  • Support the preparation of comparison summaries, evaluation scorecards and decision support material.
  • Workshops, Demos & Stakeholder Engagement
  • Help organise workshops, vendor demos and Finance stakeholder meetings.
  • Support the preparation of demo scripts and use cases relevant for Finance.
  • Capture feedback from participants and help consolidate it into structured evaluation outputs.
  • Data, Reporting & Documentation
  • Assist in preparing data samples, templates and simple analyses required during the selection phase.
  • Maintain well-structured documentation and shared repositories (requirements, decisions, risks, open points).
  • Risk, Controls & Compliance (Finance Focus)
  • Support the documentation of key Finance control and compliance aspects (e.g. segregation of duties, audit trail, closing controls), under guidance from senior staff.
  • Implementation Preparation & Support (Post Selection)
  • After ERP selection, support preparation activities for implementation (e.g. refinement of requirements, validation of design assumptions, planning of Finance-related tasks).
  • Assist in activities such as test case preparation, data preparation, and coordination with Finance users during early implementation phases.
  • Help document lessons learned and feedback from Finance stakeholders during the initial implementation steps.

Profile / Background

  • Education
  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • Technical & Analytical Skills
  • Strong analytical skills and ability to structure and synthesise information.
  • Good command of office tools, in particular spreadsheets and presentations.
  • Interest in ERP systems and Finance/Accounting processes.
  • Soft Skills
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively with Finance, IT and other stakeholders.
  • High attention to detail, reliability and good organisational skills.
  • Proactive, curious and willing to learn in a project driven environment.
  • Languages
  • Very good command of English and French (written and spoken).

Skills and experience

  • Preferred initial exposure to accounting or ERP/finance systems (e.g. through coursework, internships or student projects).
  • Basic understanding of banking or financial services.
  • Experience working in teams on projects or case studies.

What we Offer

  • Direct involvement in a strategic ERP selection and early implementation project within the Finance function.
  • Practical exposure to Finance processes, ERP solutions and project management in a banking environment.
  • Opportunity to collaborate with experienced Finance and IT professionals.
  • A learning oriented environment with regular guidance and feedback.
