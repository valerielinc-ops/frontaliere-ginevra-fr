Are you passionate about data, problem-solving, and turning complex information into clear insights?

Join our agile, self-organised team of engineers to help maintain and develop CERN's Corporate Information Systems!

Responsabilités principales

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Extract, transform, model, and visualise data in reports, dashboards, and analytics platforms;
  • Interact with relational databases, mainly Oracle, using SQL and PL/SQL;
  • Develop, maintain, and test reporting data models and semantic models supporting CERN's Finance and Procurement systems;
  • Maintain, support, and enhance existing reporting and data solutions while helping define and implement their future evolution;
  • Contribute to data profiling, mapping, quality checks, validation, and reconciliation activities during migration and transition phases;
  • Contribute to the development and maintenance of ETL/ELT pipelines and other data-processing activities where required;
  • Collaborate with business users, functional experts, architects, and developers to ensure data consistency, usability, and technical feasibility;
  • Support testing, troubleshooting, documentation, maintenance, and third-level support for reporting and data solutions.

Exigences principales

  • Data Analysis & Reporting:
  • Experience in SQL (writing, optimising, debugging complex queries).
  • Familiarity with data visualisation or analytics tools such as Power BI, R, or Python-based dashboards.
  • Understanding of data modelling principles (dimensional/star schema design, semantic models, data warehouses).
  • Experience analysing data structures, flows, and dependencies in complex enterprise systems. Data Engineering:
  • Exposure to data pipeline concepts and tools such as SQL, dbt, Airflow, or Python for data processing.
  • Interest in maintaining and modernising reporting and data solutions during a systems transformation. Business Processes & User Support:
  • Interest in providing end-user support, troubleshooting, escalation, and issue resolution.
  • Awareness of Finance and Procurement processes or interest in learning about business domains. Collaboration:
  • Experience working in group projects, ideally in collaborative or Agile environments. Skills: Data & Analytics:
  • Excellent knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
  • Knowledge of data visualisation and analytics tools, particularly Power BI.
