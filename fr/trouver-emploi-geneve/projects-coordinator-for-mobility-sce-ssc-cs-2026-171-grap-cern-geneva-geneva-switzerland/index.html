Description complète

Be part of the future of mobility at CERN and lead projects contributing to the delivery and evolution of CERN's mobility strategy. You will coordinate the implementation of the mobility plan, prepare the next mobility plan, and lead cross-functional projects and events that improve mobility across CERN sites .

Your responsibilities

  • Coordinate and monitor the actions of CERN's mobility plan through to the 2028 horizon, including milestones, dependencies, risks, decisions and follow-up actions.
  • Maintain a consolidated project portfolio and ensure regular, reliable reporting on status, priorities, issues and forthcoming decisions.
  • Work with action owners and stakeholders to translate strategic objectives into practical workplans, deliverables and implementation steps.
  • Contribute to the preparation of the future CERN mobility plan by gathering needs, analysing lessons learned, structuring consultations and drafting supporting material.
  • Pilot mobility-related projects and events, from initial scoping and stakeholder alignment through implementation, operational coordination and post-event review.
  • Participate actively in the CERN mobility working group, prepare inputs and meetings, document conclusions, and coordinate agreed follow-up.
  • Support communication and stakeholder engagement on mobility initiatives, ensuring that information is clear, timely and adapted to the target audience.
  • Identify opportunities for service improvement, sustainability and better user experience, and formulate evidence-based recommendations for decision-making.
  • Ensure that project documentation, decisions, indicators and lessons learned are recorded and accessible for continuity and governance purposes.

Your profile

  • Graduated in Project Management, Transport and Mobility, Urban Planning, Sustainability, Engineering, Business Administration, Public Policy or a related field.
  • Initial experience in project coordination, multi-stakeholder initiatives, mobility, events, sustainability or service development would be an asset.
  • Experience in an international or multidisciplinary environment would be an advantage.

Skills

  • Ability to structure and monitor projects, action plans, milestones, risks and deliverables.
  • Strong analytical and drafting skills, with the ability to turn varied inputs into concise reports and recommendations.
  • Good command of common office, collaboration, presentation and project-tracking tools.
  • Ability to organise meetings, workshops and events and to ensure systematic follow-up.
  • Interest in sustainable mobility, transport services and user-centred service improvement.
  • Clear communication and stakeholder management.
  • Ability to work autonomously while escalating risks and decisions appropriately.
  • Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the other language.

Eligibility criteria

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
  • You have a professional background in Transportation Management, Civil Engineering (or a related field) and have either:
  • a Master's degree with 2 to 6 years of post-graduation professional experience;
  • or a PhD with no more than 3 years of post-graduation professional experience.
  • You have never had a CERN fellow or graduate contract before.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Genève ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Genève ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
